\subsection{Introduction}
After everyone entered, Ward and Jacob welcomed everyone to and discussed some of the opportunities and avenues for involvement within ATU Robotics. We went around the room introducing ourselves, before Braden went through a presentation going further in detail about the club. 

\subsection{New Member Poll}
As part of the presentation, new folk were encouraged to fill out an interest form to gather some statistics regarding the demographics of the club going forward. 

There were eleven who took the poll. In terms of majors, one is in Cybersecurity, two are in Electrical Engineering, and eight are in Mechanical Engineering. Nine are freshman, one is a sophomore, and one is a junior.
