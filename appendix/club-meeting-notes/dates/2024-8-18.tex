
\section{8/18/2024 Meeting}
\subsection{Lab Organization}
The lab is significantly improved since the end of last season, though a few
things remain:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The back corner of the lab with the soldering station.
    \item The front corner of the lab with the printer.
    \item Our toolbox is completely unorganized.
    \item Our gears are unorganized.
    \item There is one remaining bucket of unsorted parts.
\end{itemize}

We would also like to develop a lab contract to place boundaries on what and how
materials and tools in the lab can be employed by other RSOs and unaffiliated
students. Additionally, there is also an air terminal that could be used to
power several fairly cheap tools that would greatly improve our productivity. We
intend to purchase a:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Air Gun
    \item Right Angle Die Grinder
    \item Cut Off Tool
    \item Reciprocating Saw
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Fundraising}
We decided to collect dues again for the year in order to purchase some new
equipment for the lab. Dues are \$25. Hunter brought up an opportunity with
Strikers bowling alley. They are going to begin opening on Sundays and as part
of a promotion are giving out discounts. If we get enough people to use these
discounts, we would be given some of the proceeds. This would require
collaboration with another RSO, to make sure we meet that goal; we intend to
work with the ATU Fishing Club.

\subsection{Involvement Fair}
The involvement fair is on August 19th. We are allowed to set up at 5:00 PM, the
event starts at 6:00 and goes until 8:00. It will be indoors at Tucker. We
prepared for the involvement fair by 3D-printing ~75 keychains of the ATU
Robotics Logo and ~75 Jerry the Bulldog miniatures. We collected the 2023-2024
15" bot, controllers, batteries, candy, the ATUM tablecloth, some trophies, and
similar paraphernalia. We will need three to four people to man our table and
talk with those interested; they should wear the ATUM shirt.

\subsection{Flyers}
Braden designed some posters for the club and we placed a few of them around
campus. The Discord server now has a verification system so that we can provide
a public invite link via a QR code.

\subsection{Visiting Students}
On August 20th there will be several freshmen visiting the lab. We will hand out
the left over items from the involvement fair and go over the club. As many club
members can come, should; they should wear the club polo. 

\subsection{First Meeting}
Our first meeting is on August 22nd. It will mostly be an introductory meeting
to those that we meet during the involvement fair and on Tuesday. A presentation
will be prepared before hand.
