\section{8/24/2023 Meeting}
This is the first team meeting for ATUM.

\subsection{The Problem}
Knowing the game rules, we've developed a list of concerns for this season
and criteria to measure the success of our robots.
Special concerns include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Division of responsibility between the two robots.
    \item Climbing on the pole with two robots.
    \item Autonomous movement with the center pole.
    \item Dealing with preloads.
    \item Launching elements across the field.
    \item Managing and making good use of double zoning.
    \item Blocking and defending against opponent element scoring.
\end{itemize}
