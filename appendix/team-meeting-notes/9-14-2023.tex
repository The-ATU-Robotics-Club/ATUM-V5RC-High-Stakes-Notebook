\subsection{Intake Arm Alternative Design}
After researching different intake designs that would be viable for our 15"
Catapult design, we came up with a "2-bar" design. This would practically be the
same thing as a chain bar, but instead of being powered by a motor it could
simply be powered with a pneumatic piston. We got motivation for this design
from VRC team 66475C \cite{bib:exmachinatwobar}.

We will take this iteration in consideration alongside the chain-bar and
four-bar.
