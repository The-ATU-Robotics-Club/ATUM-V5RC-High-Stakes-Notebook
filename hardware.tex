\subsection{11/9/24}
    Looking at the thickness of a regular high strength gear, we realize that a half thickness high strength gear would be the appropriate spacing for the gearbox.  Our first idea was to split the stl file for the gear, however we found it would cause problems with the clearance of the tip and root of the gear. We instead opted to cut a high strength 24T V2 gear in half in order to meet our spacing requirement with less compromise with our clearance. Reference \ref{fig:24T gear cuts}.
